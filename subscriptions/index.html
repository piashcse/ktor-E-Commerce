    Subscriptions & Digital Goods

    Recurring plans, invoices and digital download entitlements.

    Plans

    A plan is attached to a digital product and bills monthly or yearly.

    {
  "name": "Pro Monthly",
  "description": "Monthly access to the Pro pack",
  "productId": "product-uuid",
  "interval": "MONTH",
  "price": 9.99,
  "currency": "USD",
  "trialDays": 0,
  "isActive": true
}

    Public: GET /api/v1/subscription-plans?productId=...

    Admin CRUD:

    • GET /api/v1/admin/subscription-plans
    • POST /api/v1/admin/subscription-plans
    • PUT /api/v1/admin/subscription-plans/{id}
    • DELETE /api/v1/admin/subscription-plans/{id} — deactivates

    Endpoints

    Method Endpoint Description Authentication Required
    POST /api/v1/subscriptions Subscribe to a plan Yes
    POST /api/v1/admin/subscriptions/run-billing Create renewal invoices for due subscriptions Yes (Admin)
    POST /api/v1/admin/subscriptions/{id}/activate Admin: mark a pending subscription invoice paid and activate Yes (Admin)
    GET /api/v1/library List my digital purchases Yes
    GET /api/v1/library/{token}/download Redeem a digital download Yes

    Endpoint Details

    1. Subscribing

    POST /api/v1/subscriptions { "planId": "plan-uuid", "paymentReference": "pay_123", "paymentMethodReference": "pm_123", "providerCustomerReference": "cus_123" }

    
- With a `paymentReference`, creates an `ACTIVE` subscription with a paid invoice.
- Without a reference, creates a `PENDING` subscription and a `PENDING` invoice; no entitlement is granted until payment is recorded.
- `paymentMethodReference` / `providerCustomerReference` are **provider tokens**, not card data. They
  enable off-session renewal charges with `PAYMENT_PROVIDER=stripe`. Never send raw card numbers.
- Period starts now and ends after one month/year (plus trial days).
- Grants a digital entitlement when the plan’s product is digital and payment is confirmed.
- Rejects duplicate active subscriptions for the same plan.

Other endpoints:

- `GET /api/v1/subscriptions`
- `GET /api/v1/subscriptions/{id}/invoices`
- `POST /api/v1/subscriptions/{id}/cancel` — marks `cancelAtPeriodEnd`

### 2. Renewal billing

**`POST /api/v1/admin/subscriptions/run-billing`**
- with a stored payment method and `PAYMENT_PROVIDER=stripe` → charges the saved payment method
  off-session. A successful charge extends the period, records a `PAID` invoice, resets dunning and
  refreshes the entitlement. A `requires_action`/`processing` charge stays pending.
- without a payment method (or with the manual provider) → creates/keeps a `PENDING` invoice and
  sets the subscription `PAST_DUE` for manual collection.
- failed charges record the attempt, failure reason and next retry, and keep the subscription
  `PAST_DUE` during the 3-day grace period. After the configured attempts/grace period it becomes
  `EXPIRED` and digital access is revoked.

Returns:

```json
{ "invoicesCreated": 1, "canceled": 0, "paid": 1, "failed": 0, "expired": 0 }

    Provider renewal webhooks (Stripe payment_intent.*) are matched by the subscription_invoice_id metadata and applied idempotently; a successful webhook also marks the invoice paid and activates the subscription.

    Confirm a renewal payment manually and reactivate:

    POST /api/v1/admin/subscriptions/{id}/activate { "paymentReference": "pay_renewal" }

    
Marks the pending/failed invoice paid, advances the period, sets the subscription `ACTIVE`, resets
dunning, and refreshes the digital entitlement expiry.

## Digital goods

Products can be marked digital:

```json
{
  "isDigital": true,
  "digitalAssetUrl": "https://downloads.example/pack.zip",
  "digitalFileName": "pack.zip"
}

    Digital-only orders have zero shipping cost and the order shipping method is stored as DIGITAL. When payment completes, an entitlement is created for each digital product in the order.

    3. Library

    GET /api/v1/library [ { "entitlementId": "ent-uuid", "productId": "product-uuid", "productName": "Pro Pack", "fileName": "pack.zip", "downloadToken": "tok-uuid", "downloadCount": 0, "maxDownloads": 5, "remainingDownloads": 5, "expiresAt": "2027-05-01T00:00:00Z", "active": true, "grantedByOrder": "order-uuid", "grantedBySubscription": null } ] ```

    GET /api/v1/library/{token}/download - Rejects expired/exhausted downloads. - Increments the download count and returns a short-lived signed /api/v1/library/asset?... URL plus its expiry and remaining count. - The raw asset URL is never returned in JSON; the signed endpoint validates the HMAC/expiry and redirects to the asset.

    Subscription entitlements have a higher download limit (50) and expire at the end of the current paid period. Canceled/PAST_DUE/expired subscriptions no longer expose active downloads and their entitlements are removed on cancellation/expiry; one-off order entitlements allow 5 downloads and expire after one year. Order cancellation/full refund revokes order-based entitlements immediately.

    Other operations:

    • GET /api/v1/admin/subscriptions — List all subscriptions
